The gym by the bus stand

Mr Thunder Gym started with a simple idea: give Virudhunagar a serious, no-nonsense gym where anyone, from a first-timer to a stage competitor, can train with proper guidance.

Today it is home to strength athletes, working professionals and students who show up, train loud and keep getting stronger.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission
Make quality coaching and honest training accessible to every person who walks through the door.
Our Vision
Be the gym Virudhunagar trusts for strength, fitness and long-term health, one member at a time.
