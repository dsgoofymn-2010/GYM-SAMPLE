Our story

Serious training, proper guidance, no attitude.

Mr Thunder Gym started with a simple idea: give Virudhunagar a serious, no-nonsense gym where anyone can train with proper guidance.

First-timers train next to stage competitors. Students, working people and strength athletes share the same racks and the same coaches.

They show up, train loud and keep getting stronger.

Mission and vision

Mission
Make good coaching and honest training open to everyone who walks through the door.
Vision
Be the gym Virudhunagar trusts for strength and long-term health, one member at a time.
